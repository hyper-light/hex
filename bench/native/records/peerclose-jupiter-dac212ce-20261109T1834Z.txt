stdio_probe peerclose (native plan step 8; the lead's ruling of 2026-11-09 on the hostile-frame hang): how
soon a peer closes after the Windows node ends its connection by half-closing it. Each round the node reads
a peer's hostile request (a frame prefix past the format's largest message), answers it (62 bytes, a
refusal's size), shuts its sending side (SD_SEND, the end-of-stream) and times the peer's close; the peer, a
caller process, reads to its end and closes at once, as Hex's client and its tests do. A round whose peer
hasn't closed within the node's watch (the probe's own, 1 s; the caller's 2 s) is counted lost and reset
(linger 0), never taken as a sample. 299 rounds a run (the p99 confidence rule's N).

OF RECORD: the end-to-peer-close record, whose slowest seeds the node's learned wait for a peer's close
(Hex's rule for learned waits: twice the slowest of the record and what the node has seen since).
