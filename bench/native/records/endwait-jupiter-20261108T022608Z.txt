and faster in 94% of paired rounds. That is the pool thread's hop. Ended by TerminateProcess, a program's

1.3%, the paired rounds a coin flip, the tails noise of the load (each method has the larger in one
column). The packet also needs no thread-pool wait thread per 63 watches. By the lead's bar (the direct
packet faster is the design): the packet is the design, its ntdll calls' CLAUDE.md §12 line for Ada.
