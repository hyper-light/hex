# The lab's LAN round trips, for the membership models' one-way delay

The membership models (`crates/hex-sim/src/membership.rs`, `swim.rs`) draw each message's one-way delay
uniformly in 0.1-2 ms, a picked range (`docs/design/failure-isolation-constants.md`). Measured here to
replace it.

- adacachy → fedora, wired (adacachy's link 1,000 Mb/s), IPv6, `ping -c 600 -i 0.1`,
  2026-11-06T17:41:38Z; loads at the time: adacachy 69, fedora about 57
- 599 of 600 answered. Round trip: min 1.20 ms, p50 4.29, p90 78.1, p99 145, p99.9 192, max 207 ms
- adas-mac-studio-2 → adas-mac-studio, 2026-11-06T17:37Z: min 3.2, mean 147, max 929 ms, the Macs' wireless
  link; adas-mac-studio-3 answered none

Not adopted. Both wired ends were at load 57-70, so the tail is host scheduling delay as much as the
network's, and the models keep a slow host as its own fault (`Fault::Slow`); taking these as the LAN
would count it twice. The models' delay wants two wired hosts at rest, or the round trips a cell's own
hyper-swim learns at runtime, recorded. Until then the 0.1-2 ms range stays, kind (d), with this
reason.
